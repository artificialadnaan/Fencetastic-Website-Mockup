Pergolas

Custom Pergola Structures

Cedar provides natural beauty and durability with decay and insect resistance, making it ideal for Dallas outdoor spaces. Our pergolas are built to enhance your property with shade, style, and lasting value.

Materials & Styles:

  • Standard: Cedar posts, beams, rafters, and runners
  • Vinyl: Traditional or modern designs
  • Custom post framing and angled brace arms
  • Stone post footings for a premium finish

Patio Covers

Patio Cover Solutions

Our patio covers are customizable to fit your specific needs, designed to match your style and complement your home's architecture. Whether attached or standalone, we build structures that provide lasting shade and protection.

Configuration Types:

  • Attached or standalone structures
  • Gabled top roof option
  • Flat (sloped) top roof option
  • Shingles matching your home color
  • Customizable to fit specific needs and complement home architecture

Arbors

Entry Gate Arbors

Entry gate arbors enhance curb appeal and complement home exteriors. Available in wood or vinyl construction with arched or flat designs, our arbors add a welcoming touch to any property entrance.

  • Material Choices: Wood or vinyl construction
  • Designs: Arched or flat, custom styles available
  • Enhances curb appeal and complements home exteriors
Low Maintenance Options

Vinyl Structure Maintenance

Vinyl structures require minimal upkeep — simply clean with soap and water as needed. Vinyl resists rot, insects, and fading, giving you a beautiful outdoor structure with virtually no maintenance required.
