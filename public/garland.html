Garland Neighborhoods We Serve

Neighborhood-Specific Fencing for Garland Properties

Garland's housing stock is remarkably diverse, ranging from 1960s ranch-style homes near downtown to brand-new construction in the city's growing eastern subdivisions. This diversity means fencing needs vary widely — and Fencetastic brings the expertise to match the right materials and styles to each neighborhood's character.

Properties near Lake Ray Hubbard face unique challenges. The combination of lakeside humidity, wind exposure, and occasional flooding means fence materials must be especially weather-resistant. We recommend cedar with marine-grade hardware or vinyl for waterfront and near-water properties. The Firewheel Town Center area features newer communities with active HOAs that have specific fence requirements we know well.

The Garland ISD area is packed with family neighborhoods where privacy fencing is the top request, while the Duck Creek and Spring Creek neighborhoods offer established, tree-lined streets where fence installations need to work around mature root systems. Eastern Garland continues to grow with new subdivisions that need first-time fence installations built to modern standards. The City of Garland requires fence permits for most installations, and our team handles the entire permit process on your behalf.

  • Firewheel Town Center Area — Newer communities with active HOA guidelines we navigate daily
  • Lake Ray Hubbard Properties — Weather-resistant materials and marine-grade hardware for waterfront homes
  • Duck Creek Neighborhood — Established area with mature trees requiring careful fence installation planning
  • Spring Creek Area — Family-friendly neighborhoods with privacy fence demand
  • Eastern Garland New Subdivisions — First-time fence installation for new construction homes
  • Garland ISD Neighborhoods — Family-focused communities where 6ft privacy fencing is the most popular choice
  • Garland Permit Handling — We manage all City of Garland fence permit requirements from application to approval
