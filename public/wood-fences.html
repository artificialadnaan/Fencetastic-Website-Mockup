Materials

Wood Fence Material Comparison

We offer two wood options to fit your budget and aesthetic goals. Our team will help you choose the right material for your property.

Western Red Cedar

  • Premium choice for aesthetic appeal and longevity
  • 25-30 year lifespan with proper maintenance
  • Superior resistance to insects, decay, and warping
  • Low maintenance -- less frequent staining required
  • Available in 6 or 8-foot heights with steel posts
  • Reduced risk of splitting over time
  • Beautiful natural grain and warm color tones

Treated Pine

  • Budget-friendly alternative to cedar
  • 10-15 year lifespan with regular maintenance
  • More affordable upfront cost
  • Requires regular maintenance and staining
  • Prominent knots with a rustic aesthetic
  • Pressure-treated for ground contact durability
  • Good option for large properties on a budget
Styles

Wood Fence Styles

Choose from a variety of wood fence styles to match your property's architecture and your personal taste.

Board on Board

Overlapping vertical boards create a solid privacy fence with no gaps. Our most popular style for residential properties throughout the DFW Metroplex.

Side by Side

Adjacent vertical planks placed flush for a clean, uniform appearance. A classic and cost-effective choice for full privacy fencing.

Spaced Picket

Traditional picket design with evenly spaced boards. Perfect for front yards, garden borders, and decorative boundaries with an open feel.

Shadow Box

Alternating board pattern on both sides of the fence creates a visually appealing look from every angle while allowing airflow and natural light.

Horizontal Board on Board

Modern horizontal orientation with overlapping boards for a contemporary, sleek aesthetic. A popular choice for modern homes and updated properties.

Lattice

Decorative crisscross pattern that provides a semi-private boundary while allowing airflow and light. Often used as a fence topper or standalone accent.

Heights & Post Options

Available Heights

  • 6-foot -- Standard privacy height for residential backyards
  • 8-foot -- Maximum privacy and security for larger properties

Post Options
