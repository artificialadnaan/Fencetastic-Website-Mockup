Gate Types

Gate Styles We Build

From space-saving sliding gates to handcrafted wooden entries, we offer a full range of custom gate solutions for every property.

Sliding Gates

Space-efficient solutions ideal for properties with limited clearance. Sliding gates are safer than traditional swing gates, gliding smoothly along a track without the risk of swinging into vehicles or pedestrians.

Single & Double Swing Gates

Classic gate design with quiet operation and exceptional curb appeal. Available in multiple designs and materials, swing gates add elegance and character to any residential or commercial entrance.

Tandem Gates

Multiple gate sections that stack neatly behind one another when opened. Tandem gates are perfect for wide driveways with minimal side clearance requirements, maximizing your usable space.

Custom Wooden Gates

Handcrafted with high-quality wood, our custom wooden gates bring warmth and natural beauty to residential properties. Each gate is designed to complement your existing fence and landscape.

Why Go Automatic

Automatic Gate Benefits

Upgrade to an automatic gate system and enjoy modern convenience paired with enhanced security. Our automatic gates are built to last and designed to integrate seamlessly with your property.

  • Time-Saving Convenience — Open and close your gate from the comfort of your vehicle with a remote, keypad, or smartphone
  • Enhanced Security — Integrate keypads, remotes, cameras, and access control systems for complete property protection

Materials & Strength

Steel Gauge Specifications

We use specification-grade steel for every gate project. Lower gauge numbers mean thicker, stronger material.

Application Gauge Details
Residential 16 Gauge Ideal for everyday use — sturdy yet lightweight for smooth operation
Commercial 16 Gauge Enhanced durability designed for high-traffic areas and frequent use
Commercial Rails & Pickets 14 Gauge Maximum strength and security for demanding commercial environments
Commercial Posts 11 Gauge Increased strength for longer gates and heavy-duty applications

Note: Lower gauge = thicker, stronger steel. We select the optimal gauge for your specific project requirements.

Professional Equipment

Gate Operators

We install industry-leading gate operators with battery backup and solar options for reliable operation in any condition.

CSW24UL (Swing)

24VDC commercial swing gate operator. Handles gates up to 18 feet wide or 1,600 lbs. Battery backup and solar compatible for uninterrupted operation.

CSL24UL (Slide)

24VDC commercial slide gate operator. Powers gates up to 50 feet wide and 1,500 lbs. Designed for heavy-duty commercial and industrial applications.

Patriot II

Mechanical dual swing gate opener with a fast 12-second cycle time. Solar and AC compatible, making it ideal for residential and light commercial use.

Patriot RSL (Slide)

Slide gate operator with 1 ft/sec speed, handling gates up to 32 feet wide and 800 lbs. Battery backup and solar options included for reliable daily operation.

All operators include battery backup and solar options for reliable operation during power outages.
