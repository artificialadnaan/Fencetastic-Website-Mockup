Fort Worth Neighborhoods We Serve

Fencing for Fort Worth's Unique Neighborhoods & Properties

Fort Worth's identity blends western heritage with modern urban growth, and your fencing should reflect that character. The Stockyards District and Near Southside revitalization areas feature a mix of historic commercial properties and new residential developments, each with distinct fencing needs. Homes near the TCU campus range from charming cottages to modern student-area properties that benefit from both privacy and aesthetic appeal.

The prestigious neighborhoods of Westover Hills, Ridglea Hills, and Rivercrest feature estate-sized lots where premium fencing materials and custom gate entries make a statement. On the growth side, the Alliance corridor in far north Fort Worth is booming with new development, requiring fence installations that meet modern builder specifications and new HOA standards.

Fort Worth's western heritage means split rail and traditional farm fencing remain popular choices, especially on the city's west side toward Weatherford where rural properties and hobby farms are common. It is important to note that Tarrant County building codes differ from Dallas County in several key areas including setback requirements and height restrictions — our team knows both inside and out.
