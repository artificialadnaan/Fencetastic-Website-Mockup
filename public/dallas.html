Dallas Neighborhoods We Serve

Neighborhood-Specific Fencing Expertise Across Dallas

Dallas is a city of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own architectural character and fencing needs. The estates of Highland Park and University Park frequently require ornamental iron fencing that complements Tudor and Mediterranean-style homes, while the bungalow neighborhoods of Lakewood, Lake Highlands, and the M-Streets call for period-appropriate wood fencing that respects their early 20th-century charm.

In Preston Hollow, large lot estates often need hundreds of linear feet of premium fencing combined with custom automatic gates for driveway entries. Meanwhile, North Dallas and Far North Dallas new construction communities require fence installations that meet modern HOA standards from day one. Commercial properties in Deep Ellum and the Design District need security-focused solutions that also fit the area's creative aesthetic.

Dallas's urban heat island effect means materials heat up more than in surrounding suburbs — we recommend cedar or composite materials that handle thermal expansion without warping. Note that Dallas requires a permit for any fence over 9 feet tall, and our team handles all permit paperwork for you.

  • Highland Park & University Park — Ornamental iron and estate-grade fencing for historic properties
  • Preston Hollow — Large lot estates with premium materials and custom automatic gates
  • Lakewood & M-Streets — Period-appropriate wood fencing for Craftsman and Tudor bungalows
  • Lake Highlands — Family neighborhood privacy fences with HOA-compliant designs
  • North Dallas & Far North Dallas — New construction fence installation for modern communities
  • Deep Ellum & Design District — Commercial security fencing with creative, modern aesthetics
  • Dallas Permit Expertise — We handle all permit requirements including fences over 9 feet
