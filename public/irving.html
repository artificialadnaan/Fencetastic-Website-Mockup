Irving Neighborhoods We Serve

Fencing Solutions Tailored to Irving's Unique Areas

Irving is one of the most commercially dynamic cities in the DFW Metroplex, anchored by the Las Colinas Urban Center where major corporate campuses and office complexes require professional-grade commercial fencing, security perimeters, and access-controlled gate systems. From ornamental iron to chain link with privacy slats, we deliver commercial fencing solutions that meet corporate standards.

The Valley Ranch master-planned community is known for its strict HOA design requirements, including specific fence materials, colors, and heights. Our team has extensive experience working within Valley Ranch's guidelines to deliver fences that pass HOA review on the first submission. Meanwhile, the Toyota Music Factory entertainment district and surrounding commercial properties require durable perimeter fencing that balances security with aesthetics.

Irving's proximity to DFW Airport creates a unique need: noise reduction fencing. For properties near flight paths, we install solid board-on-board cedar fences and composite panel systems that help dampen aircraft noise. The Irving Heritage District features historic homes that benefit from period-appropriate fencing styles. North Irving and South Irving present very different property types — from newer townhome developments to established single-family neighborhoods — and commercial properties along the 635/183 corridors need security fencing built to handle high-traffic environments.

  • Las Colinas Urban Center — Commercial fencing, security perimeters, and access control for corporate campuses
  • Valley Ranch — HOA-compliant privacy fences, custom gates, and backyard solutions with strict design adherence
  • Toyota Music Factory Area — Commercial and entertainment district perimeter fencing
  • Irving Heritage District — Period-appropriate fencing for historic homes and properties
  • DFW Airport Proximity — Noise reduction fencing solutions for properties under flight paths
  • North Irving vs South Irving — Tailored solutions for different property types across the city
  • 635/183 Commercial Corridor — Security and perimeter fencing for businesses along major highways
