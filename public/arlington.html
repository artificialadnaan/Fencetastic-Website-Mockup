Arlington Neighborhoods We Serve

Fencing Solutions for Every Arlington Neighborhood

Arlington sits at the center of the DFW Metroplex, home to world-class entertainment venues like AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field, and surrounded by thriving residential neighborhoods. Properties near the entertainment district see high foot traffic and benefit from durable, attractive perimeter fencing that enhances both security and curb appeal. With no city income tax, Arlington homeowners frequently invest in property improvements like premium fencing to maximize their home's value.

Arlington's hot summers regularly push past 100°F, which means material selection matters. Western Red Cedar holds up significantly better than pressure-treated pine in extreme heat, resisting warping and splitting. Our team also engineers every installation to withstand Arlington's spring storm season — with wind-resistant post depth and concrete footings that exceed minimum code requirements.

  • Viridian — Master-planned lakeside community with specific HOA fence guidelines for materials and colors
  • Interlochen — Established neighborhood with larger lots ideal for board on board privacy fencing
  • Lake Arlington Area — Waterfront and near-water properties requiring weather-resistant fence materials
  • Dalworthington Gardens — Semi-rural properties with acreage often needing both privacy and farm fencing
  • Pantego — Tight-knit enclave community where fence style consistency matters for neighborhood character
  • Entertainment District Properties — Commercial and residential fencing near AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field
  • Standard Heights — Most Arlington neighborhoods use 6ft fences as standard, with 8ft options available for enhanced privacy along busy streets
