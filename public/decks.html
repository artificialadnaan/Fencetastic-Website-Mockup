Deck Construction Built for Texas

Whether you want a simple backyard retreat or a multi-level entertainment space, Fencetastic builds decks that last. We work with the finest materials to create outdoor living areas that withstand the Texas climate while looking stunning year after year.

Material Options

Choose Your Deck Material

We offer a range of premium decking materials to match your lifestyle, budget, and aesthetic preferences.

Trex / Composite Decking

  • Durable, rot/warp/splinter resistant
  • Low-maintenance, occasional cleaning only
  • Made from 95% recycled materials
  • Modern aesthetic, multiple colors available
  • 25-year manufacturer warranty

Pressure Treated Wood

  • Lasts long with proper care
  • Requires regular staining and sealing
  • Customizable with a variety of colors
  • Lower upfront costs for budget-friendly builds

Cedar Decking

  • Naturally resistant to rot and insects
  • Lightweight and highly workable
  • Highly durable, lasts decades
  • Less maintenance than other wood options

Stone Decking

  • Extremely durable, lifetime lifespan
  • Maintenance-free, no rotting or warping
  • Timeless natural tones and finishes
  • Eco-friendly and cost-effective long term
What We Offer

Our Deck Services

From new builds to repairs and maintenance, we handle every aspect of deck construction and care.

Simple Decks

Clean, functional designs perfect for relaxation and entertaining. A straightforward build that maximizes your outdoor space without unnecessary complexity.

Custom Decks

Tailored designs with endless possibilities. Multi-level layouts, built-in seating, lighting, and unique shapes crafted to your exact specifications.

Deck Repairs

Restoration of beauty and functionality to aging or damaged decks. We fix structural issues, replace boards, and reinforce railings to like-new condition.

Pressure Wash & Staining

Professional maintenance to extend your deck's lifespan. We pressure wash away dirt and grime, then apply premium stains and sealers for lasting protection.
