Warranty Terms

We stand behind every project we build. Learn about our warranty coverage, exclusions, and how to request service.

Our Commitment

What IS Covered

Fencetastic warranties the workmanship on your installation. If a covered defect arises, we will repair or replace the affected work at no cost to you.

Installer & Subcontractor Work

All work completed by our installers and subcontractors is fully covered under our workmanship warranty.

Alignment & Fastening

Proper alignment, secure fastening, and correct assembly are guaranteed throughout your installation.

Structural Work

Structural integrity of posts, rails, panels, and all load-bearing components is warranted against defects.

Industry Tolerances

All work is performed to standard industry tolerances as recognized by the American Fence Association.

Defective Workmanship

Any defective workmanship identified during the warranty period will be repaired or replaced at no cost to you.

Exclusions

What is NOT Covered

The following conditions are not covered under our workmanship warranty. These items are considered normal characteristics, external factors, or owner responsibilities.

  • Natural wood characteristics including shrinking, swelling, cupping, twisting, and splits under 3 inches
  • Weather damage, storm damage, and acts of nature (wind, hail, flooding, lightning)
  • Damage resulting from neglect, misuse, pets, or physical impact
  • Damage caused by removing Fencetastic signs from the installation
  • Stain fading or discoloration (stain is excluded from warranty coverage)
  • Soil movement, erosion, or changes in grade after installation
  • Work that has been modified, altered, or repaired by other contractors
  • Routine maintenance items such as gate adjustments, latch tightening, and hardware lubrication

Non-warranty service calls: Service calls that fall outside of warranty coverage are subject to a $100 trip charge. Our team will assess the issue on-site and provide a quote for any additional repairs needed.
